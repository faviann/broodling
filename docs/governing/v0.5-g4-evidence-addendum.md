## Replacement for the G4 real-provider evidence clause

G4 must support real-provider no-effect completion and substantive repair to
retained justified disposition on one coherent current product baseline. Either
one all-real integrated run supplies the complete repair path, or **all** of the
following complementary witnesses and compatibility checks must be supported.
A single all-real run never removes the independently required deterministic
controls or compatibility review in items 3–4:

1. **All-real normal product completion.** At least one actual-provider run from
   admitted original B1/Contract through actual implementation, independent
   review, adjudication, designated final assessment and normal durable
   disposition. Retain exact candidate/comparison material, raw required evidence
   and criterion rationale, readable after safe disposable cleanup.
2. **Actual downstream repair chain.** A separately labeled bounded diagnostic
   may substitute only the initial implementer with a controlled no-op, leaving
   a real, predeclared B1 Contract violation. From initial independent review
   onward, use actual qualified provider roles and unmodified product graph,
   prompts, bindings, routing and runtime. Actual review must detect a substantive
   violation; actual adjudication must issue a Contract-backed correction;
   actual repair must correct it; renewed required evidence, fresh independent
   review, explicit eligible resolution, repaired final acceptance and normal
   durable disposition/custody must follow in that same run. Independently
   substantiate the before/after violation and correction. No fabricated finding,
   directive, repair, resolution or final response; no post-admission candidate
   tampering; no model instruction to produce a deliberately bad implementation.
3. **Deterministic integrated product controls.** Existing controlled model-role
   fixtures through the actual product graph/SDK must discriminate repair
   ordering, renewed assurance, sticky/unresolved obligations, final-currentness,
   required-material/evidence gaps and strict disposition, alongside all P4
   crash/race/abandonment/replacement controls. Controlled semantic signals are
   explicitly identified and do not substitute for item 2's actual semantics.
4. **Explicit composition audit.** Pin one product source/schema/custody,
   graph/runtime, SDK/sidecar, actual provider version/settings, host/containment,
   source-placement/checkout and automatic-context configuration. Record every
   fixture substitution and relevant difference. The diagnostic implementer shim
   is a test adapter, not a qualified all-real executable or a new supported
   product profile. Verify it delegates every downstream role's prompt/arguments,
   working directory and environment unchanged to the same actual provider under
   the same containment and sandbox policies. Missing or materially incompatible
   evidence blocks the gate; affected W5/W6/W7/profile changes still require
   requalification. Do not compose incompatible positives.

Evidence composition is a human gate-review method only. No runtime combines
semantic inputs, acceptance, custody or histories from different Attempts. Each
run's disposition must be justified by its own current final SemanticAcceptance
and the same admitted Contract's explicitly empty required-effect set.

## Preserved boundaries and historical meaning

No lifecycle, source/admission, cessation, retirement, replacement, zero-carryover,
late-result isolation, interrupted-finalization, retention, authority or
no-effect rule changes. No second validator/router/ledger, semantic recovery,
candidate sealing, scheduler, session manager, automatic retry or P5 work.

Clean-only runs remain clean-only. The seeded diagnostic remains diagnostic and
its recorded `passed: false` / `integratedRealRepairWitness: false` remain true
historical statements against the prior checklist. It can support only item 2
under this new composition strategy; it is never relabeled an all-real run or
an old gate pass. Historical blocked records and issue comments stay unchanged.

This correction does not establish broad model reliability, a sensitivity
threshold, convergence rate or release readiness. G4 remains a separate fresh
skeptical read-only review after #23 is independently complete, reviewed, pushed
and closed. Record PASS only when every current obligation passes; otherwise
retain BLOCKED/FAIL and stop without product remediation inside the gate.
